US Local News: On the Front Page Front Line
The Marion County Record was raided by police in August 2023; its 98-year-old co-owner died of a heart attack the day after officers searched her home and seized the newsroom's computers. The paper kept publishing. That story sits underneath everything in this week's edition above: the local papers now straining to cover a midterm cycle are often the ones with the thinnest resources to do it, even as national outlets pour money into midterms-specific coverage like CNN's new 20/26 Vision newsletter.
